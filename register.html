Registration Information

Registration is available online or by phone at 916-888-6564. MC accepts credit card or check payments for conference registration fees.

A 30% discount is available for students attending the conference and employees of organizations sponsoring the event.

Cancellation refunds apply to both online and phone registrations:

  • Cancellations made 20 days or more before the event will receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations made 19-10 days before the event will receive a 50% refund.
  • Cancellations made 9-7 days before the event will receive a 25% refund.
  • No refunds will be issued for cancellations made less than 7 days before the event or for no-shows.
